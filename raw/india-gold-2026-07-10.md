## Import & Trade

**Duty unchanged**: India's gold import duty remains at **15%** (effective May 13, 2026 midnight notification) + 1% AIDC + 3% GST = ~19% effective total.

Clarification on earlier wiki error: India's **Union Budget 2026-27** was presented in February 2026. That budget reduced BCD from 6% to 5% (total: 5% + 1% AIDC = 6% total at that time). On **May 13, 2026**, an emergency midnight executive notification raised the gold import duty from 6% to 15%, superseding the February budget's rate. The July period has brought NO new budget or duty changes. The current 15% rate is still in effect.

Physical demand remains suppressed: imports running ~25–30t/month (down ~70% from pre-duty baseline of 75–100t/month). No new import data published today.
