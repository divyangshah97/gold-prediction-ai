# Gold Research Daily Update — 2026-05-22

## Key findings

- **Iran uranium crisis is today's dominant driver**: Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei issued a directive (May 21) that the country's near-weapons-grade uranium must not leave Iran, collapsing hopes for an imminent US-Iran peace deal. Oil surged to ~$104.52/bbl (Brent); gold slipped ~1% to ~$4,507–$4,524/oz on reduced safe-haven demand mixed with inflation fear.
- **Kevin Warsh now Fed Chair**: Confirmed by Senate (54–45) on May 13; Powell's chairmanship ended May 15. Warsh is signalling *more dovish* than initial hawkish reputation suggested — one rate cut expected by year-end. The DXY has weakened to ~99 and gold's structural tail wind from a softer dollar remains intact.
- **JPMorgan cut 2026 average gold forecast to $5,243** (from $5,708) on May 17, while maintaining its $6,000 year-end target — framing H1 as a consolidation, not a structural breakdown.
- **Goldman raised April central bank buying nowcast to ~80t/month** (vs 50t for March), lifting full-year 2026 projection to ~720t (~60t/month average). This is a significant upward revision from the already-revised 50t/month May 18 estimate.
- **Gold price consolidating in a precarious technical range** — key support cluster at $4,410–$4,430; resistance wall at $4,480–$4,500. A sustained break below $4,362 would open deeper losses.

## Other geopolitical risks

**Iran — active developing story (highest priority):**
- **May 21 (yesterday)**: Iran's new Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei issued a directive that near-weapons-grade uranium must remain on Iranian soil. This directly contradicts a core US demand — the uranium disposition question is the central sticking point in nuclear talks.
- Trump called off imminent airstrikes at Gulf Arab allies' request but threatened Wednesday to resume military action if Iran does not give "100% good answers" within "a couple more days."
- The US-Iran ceasefire (agreed ~late April, ~5+ weeks old) is fragile. The Strait of Hormuz remains at just 5% of pre-conflict shipping traffic.
- Brent crude surged to ~$104.52/bbl (+1.9%) on May 22; WTI at ~$97.81/bbl (+1.5%). Oil above $100 is stoking inflation fears, pressuring the Fed, and creating a complex push-pull for gold (inflation = bullish; higher rates risk = bearish).
- Background: The 2026 Iran war began February 28 with US-Israeli strikes. Ali Khamenei (the father) and Larijani were killed.

## Macro factors

**Federal Reserve — major structural change:**
- Kevin Warsh confirmed as 17th Fed Chair by Senate (54–45 vote) on May 13; Powell's chairmanship ended May 15. The Federal Reserve Board named Powell "chair pro tempore" until Warsh was sworn in.
- Powell stays on as a governor (term runs to January 2028) pending completion of an investigation into Fed headquarters construction project handling.
- **Warsh's policy posture**: Initially flagged as hawkish (gold fell 16% intraday on the January 30 nomination announcement), but April 2026 confirmation hearings showed a more dovish tone. He has signalled willingness to cut rates earlier than consensus — and is seen as broadly USD-negative.
- **Fed funds rate**: Held at 3.5–3.75% at the April 29 meeting (3rd consecutive hold). First rate cut now potentially expected H2 2026 under Warsh.
- **Inflation**: Elevated, partly driven by energy prices from the Iran conflict (oil at $97–104/bbl). Still above the 2% target.
- **USD (DXY)**: Around 99 on May 21–22, pulling back from a six-week high.
