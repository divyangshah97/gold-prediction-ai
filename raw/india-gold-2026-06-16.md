## Import & Trade

No new policy changes. Import duty remains at **15%** (10% BCD + 5% AIDC), effective May 13, 2026 — the steepest single duty increase on record, fully reversing the July 2024 duty cut. Additional 3% GST on gold transactions.

WGC estimates the 15% duty will reduce India 2026 demand by ~50-60 tonnes (-10% y/y). Q1 2026 imports were 186t (+58% YoY) — frontloading ahead of the duty hike. Import volumes expected to decline sharply in Q2-Q4 2026.

**Unofficial gold smuggling risk**: Historical data shows higher duty correlates with higher unofficial gold imports (smuggling). RBI implemented additional cash-only purchase restrictions. Dubai-India route is the primary watch.

## ETF & Investment Flows

**June 2026 recovery**: Gold ETF inflows rebounded to **+₹2,081 crore** in June 2026 (as of mid-June), recovering from May 2026's first outflow in 13 months (₹725 crore outflow — profit booking amid sharp price correction from ₹1,57,000+ to ₹1,47,000+ range).

**AMC caps still in force** (no lift): Six AMCs (HDFC, ICICI Prudential, Nippon, Tata, Aditya Birla Sun Life, and at least one more) are capping large gold ETF subscriptions:
- Direct subscription caps: ₹25 crore per transaction
- FoF route caps: ₹10 lakh per PAN per month
- Effective June 8-9, 2026; status "until further notice"

Aditya Birla Sun Life became the 6th AMC to impose caps (effective June 9, 2026). These caps prevent institutional arbitrage between MCX and gold ETFs but are creating friction for large investors seeking gold exposure. Small retail investors (SIPs) are largely unaffected.

**Global context**: WGC May 2026 ETF report title: "The West returns to the fold" — Western gold ETF inflows recovered in May 2026 while Eastern inflows (India/China) saw short-term outflows.
