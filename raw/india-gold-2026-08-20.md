## MCX Gold Price

MCX gold was volatile on August 19, 2026: it eased to ~₹1,54,109/10g earlier in the session (Sunday Guardian Live) but then rallied strongly into the evening close, reclaiming above ₹1.56 lakh/10g — Goodreturns reported an intraday jump to ₹1,56,626/10g (+₹2,364, +1.53%). Separate city-wise retail readings for the day showed 24K at ~₹1,54,970/10g, 22K at ~₹1,42,050/10g, and 18K at ~₹1,16,230/10g (an earlier snapshot, before the evening rally). Silver was reported around ₹2,30,260/10g by one tracker (StartupTalky) amid a broader silver slide, while Goodreturns cited MCX silver above ₹2.34 lakh/kg. The wide intraday range and disagreement between trackers reflects the sharp global COMEX rally (XAU/USD +2.3% on the day) landing on top of already-volatile domestic pricing. This tracks the same global rally captured in `prices/prices.csv` (XAU/USD close $4,491.71, +2.32% vs Aug18's $4,390.00).

## ETF & Investment Flows
