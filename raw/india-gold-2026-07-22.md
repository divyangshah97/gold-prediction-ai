## Import & Trade

**June 2026 gold imports**: $1.97 billion (down **42% month-on-month**; lowest since June 2025). Imports have been suppressed by:
1. Iran Strait of Hormuz disruption affecting Dubai→India gold routing
2. Domestic price sensitivity at elevated levels (15% import duty unchanged)
3. Subdued consumer demand after the sharp price run-up in H1 2026

Full fiscal year FY2025-26 gold import bill: $71.98 billion (record). **Import duty**: 15% (unchanged since August 2024 reduction from 15% to 6%, then restored to 15% in June 2025). WGC estimates the 15% duty will reduce India's gold demand by 50–60 tonnes in 2026.

**Gem & jewellery exports** (June 2026): +26% YoY (GJEPC data, Business Standard July 20). Gold leads export growth, signalling strong international demand for Indian crafted jewellery even as domestic consumer demand cools.

## Retail & Jewellery Demand

Consumer demand remains **mixed/subdued** overall. The WGC India Gold Market Update (July 2026) notes "mixed demand signals":
- Jewellery buying picked up modestly after the steep price fall in June (from $5,405 peak to ~$4,002 low)
- Bargain-hunting drove some uptick in discretionary purchases in tier-2 and tier-3 cities
- However, overall demand remains below pre-price-run-up levels
- For the first time in 2026, **investment demand (82t) has surpassed jewellery demand (66t)** — a structural shift toward formal financial channels

Gold discounts in India (wholesale): narrowing from $150/oz peak discount in May 2026 to approximately $20/oz in July 2026, indicating physical demand recovery.
