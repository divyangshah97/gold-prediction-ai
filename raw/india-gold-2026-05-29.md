## Import & Trade

Import duty structure (unchanged since May 13, 2026):
- Basic Customs Duty (BCD): 10%
- Agriculture Infrastructure and Development Cess (AIDC): 5%
- Total effective duty: 15%
- Previous rate: 6%

The duty hike was implemented midnight May 13 by government notification. Stated rationale: protect INR amid West Asia conflict; conserve FX reserves; curb non-essential imports. Resulted in immediate MCX price spike on May 13. The WGC expects the hike to reduce annual gold demand by ~50–60 tonnes.

April 2026 import data: India gold ETF net demand in Q1 2026 was record 20 tonnes (INR 316bn / ~$3.45bn); January 2026 alone recorded 80% of Q1 inflows.
