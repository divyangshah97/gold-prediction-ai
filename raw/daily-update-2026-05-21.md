## US-China & trade war

This formulation is non-binding, defines no specific controls to lift, sets no timeline, and creates no verification mechanism.

- Both sides are weighing matching $30 billion tariff cuts — talks ongoing as of May 20
- Rare earth export controls on 12 elements remain in force
- Chinese exports of rare earths remain ~50% below pre-control levels
