## Import & Trade

The import duty hike from 6% to **15%** (BCD raised from 5% to 10%; AIDC simultaneously raised) effective **May 13, 2026** continues to dominate India's gold import dynamics:
- India imported gold/silver worth **$84 billion in FY26** (10.8% of total merchandise imports)
- April 2026 imports: **$5.6 billion** (up ~80% y/y and sequentially) — note: this was pre-duty-hike demand pull-forward
- Post-hike (fortnight ended May 27): gold demand fell to ~**7.5 tonnes** vs ~25 tonnes in the same period last year — a **70% decline**
- Domestic gold trading at a **$87/oz discount** to international benchmarks as of early June (duty cost wedge)
- Full-year 2026 demand estimated to decline ~50–60 tonnes (~10% y/y) across jewellery and bar/coin
- WGC caution: elevated import duties historically revive illegal gold smuggling via Dubai and other routes

## Retail & Jewellery Demand

Sharply lower. PM Modi's May 10, 2026 public appeal to defer non-essential gold purchases remains in force. The 70% demand collapse in the post-duty-hike fortnight reflects genuine consumer demand destruction, not just pull-forward reversal. India's gold-sensitive festive calendar (Akshaya Tritiya passed in April 2026) means next major demand window is Navratri/Dhanteras in October-November 2026.

## ETF & Investment Flows

**Gold ETFs recorded their FIRST net outflow in 13 months in May 2026**: ₹725 crore net outflow, ending a 13-month inflow streak. Described as "classic profit-booking after a sustained rally." Context: global gold ETFs also saw slight net withdrawals in May. Four major AMCs (HDFC, Nippon, ICICI Prudential, Tata) continue to cap gold ETF inflows (₹10 lakh/PAN/month for FoF; ₹25 crore direct cap). This is the first simultaneous multi-AMC gold ETF restriction in India's history.
