## Retail & Jewellery Demand
No new daily data point, but World Gold Council's Q2 2026 India Focus report (most recent available) shows volume demand softened 6% y/y to 131.4 tonnes while value spending hit a Q2 record of ₹1.98 lakh crore (+50% y/y) on elevated prices. Consumers are shifting toward lighter, lower-carat (18K/14K) pieces and smaller, less frequent purchases, aided by exchange offers, gold savings schemes, and EMIs. WGC and market commentary (Nuvama, others) expect the wedding and festive season — beginning late August 2026 — to support demand, with retailers reported to be building inventory ahead of it, though elevated prices and a weak monsoon remain headwinds. (source: gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-demand-trends/gold-demand-trends-india-focus-q2-2026)
