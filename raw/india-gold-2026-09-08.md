## MCX Gold Price

MCX gold futures traded choppy through the session: down ~0.01% to ₹1,52,750/10g at one point, having briefly crossed ₹1.54 lakh (+~1%) earlier in the session before paring gains. Prices opened the week lower on better-than-expected US jobs data (raising Fed rate-hike odds) but pared losses later as the dollar slipped, making gold more attractive.
