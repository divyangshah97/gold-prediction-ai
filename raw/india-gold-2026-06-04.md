## RBI / Reserves

**MAJOR STORY: Bloomberg RBI Gold-Sale Claim — DENIED**

On June 2, 2026, Bloomberg Economics published an analysis claiming India's RBI may have offloaded ~$12 billion
in gold holdings (approximately 85–90 tonnes at current prices) during the two weeks ending May 22, 2026,
simultaneously purchasing ~$7.5 billion in foreign-currency assets, to shield FX reserves from Middle East war fallout.

**RBI officially denied this**: The Reserve Bank of India issued a statement confirming physical gold holdings remain
unchanged at 880.52 metric tonnes. The RBI stated gold holdings rose from 880.34t on March 27 to 880.52t on April 3
— showing marginal *increase*, not decline.

**Government fact-check**: India's PIB (Press Information Bureau) called the Bloomberg report "fake" and cited:
- Gold's share in India's FX reserves: 13.92% (September 2025) → 16.70% (March 31, 2026) → 16.85% (May 22, 2026)
- The rising share of gold in reserves contradicts any large-scale gold sale hypothesis.
- The Centre noted reserves rose to 16.85% as of May 22, the date Bloomberg cited for alleged gold sales.

**Assessment**: The Bloomberg analysis appears based on balance-sheet inference from publicly available data and may
conflate valuation effects (gold price moves) with volume changes. The government/RBI denial with specific percentage
data (16.85% gold share on the exact date in question) is strong. No further corroboration of sales found.

**Current RBI gold status**: 880.52t unchanged (confirmed April 3, 2026 data). No new buying since Q1 2026 (marginal ~0.34t).
Repatriation programme (168t from BoE) ongoing.
