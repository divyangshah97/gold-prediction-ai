## Import & Trade

India gold imports remain down ~70% in volume to 25–30t/month (official government confirmation June 18, 2026) following the 15% import duty effective May 13, 2026. In value terms, May 2026 imports were $3.41B (+34% y/y) — high prices keep forex outflow elevated despite volume compression.

FY26 gold imports: record USD 71.98 billion. The government's import duty hike was explicitly designed to arrest rupee slide and compress import volumes; objective partially met on volumes, less so on forex value.

100 kg cap on duty-free imports under Advance Authorisation scheme remains in effect.

## Retail & Jewellery Demand

Weak. Jewellery purchases remain sluggish due to: (1) typical slow seasonal period for June, (2) sharp price volatility creating uncertainty, (3) elevated prices 13.2% above pre-duty-hike levels in domestic terms, (4) PM Modi's May 10 public appeal to defer non-essential gold purchases for at least one year. Reduced store traffic across major consuming cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad). Gold demand expected to decline 50–60 tonnes (~10% y/y) for full year 2026.

## ETF & Investment Flows

May 2026 saw a record **₹725 crore outflow** — first monthly net outflow from gold ETFs in 13 months (April 2025 was the last outflow). June 2026 is seeing recovery with estimated inflows of ~₹2,081 crore (rebounding from May weakness). 6 AMC subscription caps (Nippon, HDFC, ICICI Prudential, Tata, UTI Gold ETF FoF) remain in effect for large subscriptions. Total gold ETF AUM was ₹1.71 lakh crore at FY26 end (March 2026; +191% y/y); now moderating on price correction and policy environment.
