## MCX Gold Price

MCX gold futures traded at approximately **₹1,41,588 per 10 grams** on July 15, 2026 — down ~0.50% (−₹710) from the previous close. Intraday range: ₹1,41,200–₹1,41,800. Silver on MCX at approximately ₹2.22 lakh/kg.

MCX gold remained under pressure despite soft US CPI data (released July 14, showing June CPI 3.5% vs 4.2% prior), as rupee weakening and cautious sentiment weighed. Physical gold rates in major cities: 24K Delhi ₹14,158/g; Mumbai ₹14,110/g.

## RBI / Reserves

No new RBI gold data. RBI reserves remain at **880.52 tonnes** (Q1 2026), accounting for ~17.2% of India's foreign exchange reserves — a record high proportion. Repatriation of 168t from Bank of England (London) is ongoing but no new update today.

## Import & Trade

Import duty remains at **15% (from 6% pre-May 13, 2026)**. WGC estimated the duty hike will reduce India's annual gold demand by **50–60 tonnes** (~10% year-on-year) in 2026. India's gold import bill reached a record **USD 71.98 billion in FY26** prior to the duty change. No new policy announcement today.

## Retail & Jewellery Demand

Physical gold rates in major cities jumped on account of rupee weakening (USD/INR ~₹96.40) even as MCX futures fell. Jewellery demand typically slows during summer months ahead of the festive (Navratri/Diwali) season pickup in Oct–Nov. WGC data shows India gold demand cooled in Q2 2026 following the duty hike.
