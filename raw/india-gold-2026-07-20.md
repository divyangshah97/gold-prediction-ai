## Import & Trade
June 2026 gold imports: **$1.97 billion** (down 42% month-on-month; lowest since June 2025). The sharp drop reflects: (1) Iran Strait of Hormuz closure disrupting Dubai–India shipment routes; (2) high domestic gold prices suppressing discretionary demand; (3) 15% import duty in effect since May 13, 2026, acting as a structural demand dampener. WGC India update: "demand cools" — gold imports have declined for two consecutive months. Annual FY26 import bill remains at record $71.98B.

## Retail & Jewellery Demand
Demand continues to soften. Indian retail jewellery demand remains approximately −19% year-on-year in volume terms (prior data; no fresh July 20 release). The 15% import duty has compressed dealer margins and consumer purchasing. The WGC "India gold market update: Mixed demand signals" (July 2026) noted: digital gold purchases via UPI rebounded in June (+9% m/m volume, +4% m/m value to ₹25.5B), but physical demand remains subdued. Price pullback from ₹155,000+ (April highs) to ₹141,000 range has attracted some bargain hunters.

## ETF & Investment Flows
June 2026: Gold ETF net inflows of **₹34.4 billion (US$356mn)** — highest since February 2026. Holdings increased by 2.2t to **119t**. Cumulative AUM fell ~8% month-on-month due to lower gold prices (price effect, not flow effect). ETF investors viewed the price correction as a buying opportunity. July 1–10 estimated inflows: **₹12.1 billion (US$127mn)** — tracking to another inflow month. For context, May 2026 saw the first monthly outflow in 13 months (₹725 crore net outflow), now reversed.
