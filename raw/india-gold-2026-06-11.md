## Import & Trade

**Import duty**: Remains at **15%** (10% BCD + 5% AIDC), effective since May 13, 2026. No change.

**Demand destruction confirmed**: Physical gold demand remains ~70% below year-ago levels. WGC projects full-year 2026 demand decline of ~50–60t (~10% y/y). Jewellery and bar/coin channels most affected. Smuggling risk elevated at 15% duty.

**June CPI impact on imports**: May US CPI at 4.2% raises WTI (higher oil = higher India energy import bill = more pressure on current account = more INR stress). However today's ₹95.26 is slightly stronger than yesterday's ₹95.36.

## ETF & Investment Flows

**ETF restrictions remain in force**: HDFC, Nippon India, ICICI Prudential, Tata AMC all capping gold ETF inflows (₹10 lakh/PAN/month for FoF; ₹25 crore cap on direct subscriptions). No new AMC adding or lifting restrictions as of June 11.

**Q1 2026 data** (confirmed): India accounted for 32% of all global gold ETF demand in Q1 2026. Net inflows: INR 316bn (US$3.45bn), adding ~20t to total holdings — strongest quarterly inflows ever recorded for India ETFs.

**Structural shift**: Physical demand channel suppressed; digital gold/ETF channel also now facing AMC-level restrictions. Investors are shifting to SGB (Sovereign Gold Bonds) and Goldhub digital products as alternatives.

## Other India Gold News

**Iran MOU status**: US and Iran negotiators have reached tentative agreement on a 60-day MOU (Hormuz reopening, mines cleared, Iran no nuclear weapons pledge, US sanctions waivers and port blockade lift). Trump has not yet signed; deal awaiting finalization as of June 11. On June 10, US struck Iranian radar/drone sites, Iran launched missiles in Gulf region. MOU progress, if completed, would reduce oil prices → improve India's current account → support INR → reduce domestic gold price upward pressure.

**US-China trade deal (June 11)**: New 60-day deal announced by Trump. Keeps 30% tariffs (20% fentanyl + 10% reciprocal) but pauses higher tariffs. For India: reduces global risk aversion somewhat, which is slightly INR-positive but also reduces safe-haven gold demand. India gold/precious metals explicitly excluded from forced labor tariff scope.

**May US CPI 4.2%**: The highest inflation print since April 2023. Driven by 23.5% energy surge (Iran/Hormuz crisis). Confirms US inflation trajectory remains elevated → Fed rate hike risk → Gold bearish macro environment.
