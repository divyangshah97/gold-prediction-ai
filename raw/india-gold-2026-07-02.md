## Import & Trade
India raised gold import duty to 15% effective May 13, 2026 (Basic customs duty doubled from 5% to 10%; AIDC cess raised from 1% to 5%). Govt also capped duty-free imports under Advance Authorisation scheme at 100 kg. This is the steepest single adjustment to India's gold import duty on record. The duty hike was motivated by rupee depreciation (>7% YTD at time of hike) and pressure on forex reserves. Official gold imports fell approximately 70% to 25-30t/month as a result.

## ETF & Investment Flows
