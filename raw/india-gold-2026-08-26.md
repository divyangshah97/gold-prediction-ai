## MCX Gold Price
MCX gold up ~0.84% at roughly ₹1.63 lakh/10g (24K ≈ ₹16,375/gram) on Aug 26, consistent with the established ₹1.63-1.65 lakh/10g range tracking the COMEX rally (Aug25 close $4,675.96, a fresh 3-month+ high). Market described as "highly volatile"; no major new level breach beyond the established baseline (source: businesstoday.in, sundayguardianlive.com).

## RBI / Reserves
No new data. Reserves remain static at 880.52t through Q2 2026, unchanged from Q1 (~$102.6bn as of June 2026). No new buying, selling, or repatriation announcements found today (source: tradingeconomics.com/india/gold-reserves).

## Import & Trade
No rate change: 15% duty regime (10% BCD + 5% AIDC, in force since May 13, 2026, the steepest hike on record from 6%) unchanged. New data point: government collected ₹10,040 crore in gold-import customs duty between May 13 and Aug 2, 2026 — the first ~2.5 months of the new regime (source: business-standard.com).

## Retail & Jewellery Demand
WGC reports demand recovery strengthening ahead of the festive season (starts late Aug, peaks at Dhanteras/Diwali Oct-Nov). Jewellery buying has picked up since late June on lower/stabler prices; retailers building inventory, manufacturers reporting higher order flows. Elevated prices (international +9% in first two weeks of August to $4,391/oz; domestic +7% to ₹1,51,744/10g by Aug 14) remain a headwind even as investment demand stays supportive (source: gold.org, tribuneindia.com, knnindia.co.in).

## ETF & Investment Flows
India gold ETFs pulled in ₹1,179 crore net inflows in the first half of August 2026 despite the price rebound — a continuation of the previously-confirmed figure, following July 2026's ₹1,560 crore (itself 55% lower m/m than June), suggesting inflows continuing but at a moderating pace (source: businesstoday.in).

## INR Impact
USD/INR around ₹95.4-95.9 in late August 2026, roughly in line with the established ₹95.73 baseline (Aug25 close basis: ₹95.7310, -0.04% vs Aug24). No sharp move; a weak rupee continues to add a modest premium to domestic gold vs. international benchmarks (source: bookmyforex.com).

## Policy & Regulation
Reports indicate government preparing to relaunch a revised Gold Monetisation Scheme "within the next two weeks," targeting India's estimated 25,000 tonnes of idle household gold. Scheme currently only accepts deposits under the Short-Term Gold Deposit (STGD) category (1-3 years, min. 10g). Still in active discussion among RBI/Finance Ministry/industry rather than formally notified as of Aug 26 — consistent with baseline, but momentum toward a near-term announcement is new (source: vblgoldfix.substack.com, whalesbook.com).

## Other India Gold News
India's net gold recycling dropped to an 11-quarter low of 19 tonnes in Q1 FY27 (-38% QoQ, -17% YoY), partly attributed to high prices discouraging households from selling. MMTC-PAMP (India's sole LBMA-accredited gold/silver refiner) is lobbying for duty parity ahead of Union Budget 2026, and separately plans a silver recycling pilot in Delhi within 3-4 months plus minting-capacity expansion (2.4M→3.6M coins) over five years (source: sahi.com, outlookbusiness.com).
