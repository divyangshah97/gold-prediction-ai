## MCX Gold Price
MCX gold futures traded down ~0.88% at approximately ₹1,42,000 per 10 grams intraday on July 29, 2026, tracking the overnight COMEX pullback (Gold fell to $4,026.33 on July 28, -1.25% day-over-day) and a firmer US dollar ahead of today's FOMC decision (source: BusinessToday, FXStreet). Silver crossed ₹2.15 lakh/kg domestically even as gold eased, reflecting continued outperformance of silver relative to gold in the current leg (source: BusinessToday).

## RBI / Reserves
No new RBI gold purchase/sale data since the last update — reserves remain at the previously reported ~881 tonnes (April 2026 print; source: tradingeconomics.com/india/gold-reserves). Separately, a July 14 report notes RBI has been cutting its US Treasury holdings to a six-year low while continuing to diversify reserves, with gold accumulation cited as part of that broader diversification strategy — this is a restatement of an already-tracked trend, not a new tonnage announcement (source: organiser.org, July 14).

## Import & Trade
No new India gold import/duty policy data since the last update. The 15% basic customs duty (raised from 6% on May 13, 2026) remains in effect; WGC continues to project a 50-60 tonne (~10% y/y) hit to full-year jewellery and bar/coin demand from the higher duty (source: World Gold Council, prior tracking — no change today).

## Retail & Jewellery Demand
World Gold Council's latest India market update (dated July, "mixed demand signals") notes overall consumer demand remained subdued in June, though jewellery buying reportedly picked up after the steep price fall, and lower prices have been supporting jewellery purchases, ETF inflows, and digital gold demand simultaneously (source: gold.org, India market update).

## ETF & Investment Flows
Indian gold ETFs continued net inflows into July: ₹12.1bn (~$127mn) net inflows recorded 1-10 July, with 135,000 new folios added during the month (12.5mn total accounts) — continuing June's rebound of ₹34.4bn (~$356mn), the strongest month since February, as investors treated the price pullback as a buying opportunity (source: World Gold Council).

## INR Impact
USD/INR closed around ₹95.74 (previous session, July 28), broadly flat day-over-day but down (rupee weaker) ~1.17% over the past month and ~9.98% over the past 12 months (source: tradingeconomics.com/india/currency). A weaker rupee continues to provide a partial offset to domestic (MCX) gold prices even when COMEX/XAU-USD softens.

## Policy & Regulation
No new India gold policy announcements in the last 24-48h beyond the already-tracked 15% import duty and RBI reserve-diversification stance (source: as above).
