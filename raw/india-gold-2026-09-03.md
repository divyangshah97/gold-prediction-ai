## Import & Trade

A notable new angle surfaced: India's gold-bar imports from the **UAE** have risen sharply, attributed to a **1 percentage-point tariff gap** under the India-UAE Free Trade Agreement — FTA-qualifying gold enters at 14% versus the standard 15% MFN rate — making the UAE route the cheapest legal import channel and pulling volumes away from other suppliers. Separately (background, not new today): the 15% import duty (hiked from 6% in May 2026) remains in place; India's average monthly gold imports rose to ~83 tonnes in early 2026 vs ~53 tonnes in 2025; a government review of cutting the duty back toward 6% (flagged Sept2, smuggling-concerns-driven) remains under consideration with no decision announced.
