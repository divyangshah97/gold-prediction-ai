## Import & Trade

- Import duty: Raised from 6% to 15% in a "midnight" announcement on May 13, 2026 — the sharpest single increase ever recorded (source: BusinessToday, CNBC)
- Stated reason: India imports most gold in USD; high gold demand forces rupee-to-dollar conversion, weakening the INR; duty hike aims to arrest the rupee slide and narrow the trade deficit
- PM Modi made a direct public appeal on May 10, 2026, urging citizens to defer gold purchases for at least one year
- WGC estimate: India's gold demand likely to decline 50–60 tonnes in 2026 (~10% y/y), primarily from jewellery and bar/coin channels
- Smuggling risk: WGC cautioned that the 15% duty could revive illegal gold imports, a recurring issue historically when duties were high
- India gold imports April 2026: Data not yet released; duty hike took effect May 13 so April data unaffected
