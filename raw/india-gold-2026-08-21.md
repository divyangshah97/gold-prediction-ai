## MCX Gold Price
MCX gold futures traded firmly higher on August 21, tracking the overnight COMEX rally. August/near-month gold futures rose ~0.36-0.52% to around **₹1,60,250/10g** (Analytics Insight), with Business Standard reporting a **₹1,205** rise on the day (safe-haven demand cited). Sunday Guardian Live's retail snapshot puts round-number MCX gold at **~₹1.6 lakh/10g**, crossing above the ₹1.59 lakh mark reported earlier in the day by Business Today. Retail 24K quotes ranged **₹15,928-16,048/gram** (~₹1,59,280-1,60,480/10g) depending on source/timing; 22K ~₹14,601/gram; 18K ~₹11,947/gram (source: sundayguardianlive.com, businesstoday.in, chshyd.in, analyticsinsight.net).

MCX September silver opened **₹2,44,988/kg**, up ₹1,745 from the prior close of ₹2,43,243 (Business Standard), while a later intraday read showed September silver futures down 1.02% to ₹2,45,736 — sources diverge on direction magnitude but agree silver held around/above the **₹2.55 lakh/10g** retail level (source: business-standard.com, sundayguardianlive.com).

International reference: Gold (COMEX/spot proxy via TradingEconomics) rose to **$4,554.76/oz**, up 0.86% on the day, consistent with the Aug 20 US-session close of $4,516.60 carried into today. Silver eased slightly to **$67.97/oz**, down 0.15% (source: tradingeconomics.com/commodity/gold, tradingeconomics.com/commodity/silver). Domestic MCX gains tracked this international move plus rupee stability (see INR Impact below).

## Import & Trade
No new duty/policy change; the 15% import duty regime (effective since May 13, 2026) remains unchanged. Most recent confirmed trade data (July 2026, still the latest available): gold imports rose **4.77% m/m to $4.16 billion** (Daily Pioneer, source dated for the July print), consistent with WGC's earlier-cited **40-45 tonnes** July volume figure (up from 20t in June) reflecting inventory replenishment ahead of the festive season. No fresher August 21-specific demand survey found beyond this WGC commentary.

## Policy & Regulation
No formal government announcement of a revamped Gold Monetisation Scheme (GMS) as of August 21, but pressure for reform intensified: MMTC-PAMP publicly renewed its call for a redesigned GMS/recycling framework, urging better consumer incentives to mobilize India's estimated **31,000 tonnes of household gold** and reduce reliance on ~800 tonnes/year of imports, noting its refineries operate far below capacity (300t/year gold capacity vs. ~22t currently processed); India's net gold recycling fell to an 11-quarter low of 19 tonnes in Q1 FY27, down 38% q/q and 17% y/y (source: sahi.com/news/mmtc-pamp-calls-for-better-gold-recycling-schemes-to-unlock-household-stock, millenniumpost.in). Separately, earlier (July 2026) reporting indicated the government was preparing a revamped GMS — for the first time allowing jewellers to act as "collection partners" — with an announcement expected ahead of the festive season, but no official notification has landed yet as of this date (source: vblgoldfix.substack.com/p/india-to-relaunch-gold-monetization, freepressjournal.in/business/govt-may-revamp-gold-monetisation-scheme-report). This remains an unconfirmed/pending policy item, not yet enacted.
