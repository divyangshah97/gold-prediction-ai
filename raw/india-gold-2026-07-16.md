## Import & Trade

Import duty remains at 15% (effective May 13, 2026). WGC July 1 report estimated the duty could reduce 2026 gold demand by **50–60t** (approximately 10% y/y). India's gold ETF AUM has reached ₹1.84T (May 2026) but AMC subscription caps remain in force for large investors. No new import policy changes today.

Import volumes remain suppressed (~25-30t/month vs 75-100t/month pre-duty). In value terms, April 2026 imports were ~15t (near 30-year low) as ETF sourcing pressured physical pipeline.

## Retail & Jewellery Demand

Retail demand continues to be suppressed by the 15% import duty and PM Modi's appeal to defer purchases. WGC forecast for 2026: jewellery + bar + coin demand expected to fall 50-60t (~10% y/y). Jewellery sector saw 8% volume decline in FY26. FY27 projected additional 13-15% decline to ~620-640t. Gold remains affordable in nominal terms but 15% duty creates substantial premium over international prices.

## INR Impact

USD/INR: **₹96.19** on July 16 vs ₹96.40 on July 15 — rupee firmed slightly (INR appreciated 0.22%). This slightly reduces the INR cost of gold imports but the 15% duty still dominates. Year-to-date INR depreciation: ~7% from ~₹89.5 start-2026.

At ₹96.19 and 15% duty + 3% GST (~18.5% effective duty):
- Goldman GS target $4,900 → MCX implied ~**₹1,70,540/10g** (domestic currently ~17.3% below GS year-end target)
- JPMorgan Q4 target $4,500 → MCX implied ~**₹1,56,630/10g** (domestic currently ~10.6% below JPM Q4)

The domestic gap to institutional year-end targets suggests significant upside potential if macro conditions normalize and duty is revised.
