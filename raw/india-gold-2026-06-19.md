## Import & Trade

**MAJOR CONFIRMED DATA — Government official statement June 18, 2026:**

India's gold imports have **dropped ~70% in volume terms** to **25–30 tonnes per month** following the May 13, 2026 import duty hike (from 6% to 15%).

| Metric | Pre-duty (Jan–Apr 2026) | Post-duty (May–June 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly import volume | 75–100 tonnes/month | 25–30 tonnes/month |
| Volume decline | — | **~70%** |
| May 2026 import value | — | **$3.41 billion** (−34% y/y... wait) |

Note: In VALUE terms, May 2026 gold imports were $3.41 billion (+34% y/y), driven by exceptionally high gold prices. The duty hike suppressed VOLUMES but elevated PRICES mean VALUE remained high. This is a policy success in terms of volume suppression but partial in terms of forex outflow reduction.

Government source (ANI, June 18): "Import duty on gold works: Official says volumes down to 25-30 tonnes/month." Effective May 13, import duty raised to 15% from 6%. Combined with the Agriculture Infrastructure Development Cess (AIDC) and 3% GST, the effective landed cost premium is substantial.

Key concerns: Historical precedent (2013 4% duty hike) shows smuggling rises sharply within 6-12 months. WGC India head Kavita Chacko warned in May 2026 update that "import tightening" may accelerate grey-market channels.
