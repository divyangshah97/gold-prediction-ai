## Retail & Jewellery Demand

July 1 marks the beginning of Q3 2026. India is in a seasonal lull — the post-wedding/festival season trough typically runs June–August before picking up in September (Navratri/Dhanteras). At current MCX prices (~₹1,41,000/10g), jewellery demand remains suppressed versus historical averages. Consumer sentiment: cautious. High prices + duty = strong disincentive for discretionary purchases. Investment demand (bars, coins, ETF) continues to outperform jewellery on a relative basis.
