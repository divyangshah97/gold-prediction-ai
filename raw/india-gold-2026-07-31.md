## Retail & Jewellery Demand

WGC Q2 2026 India Gold Demand Trends (published this week) shows demand fell 6% y/y to 125.4t in Q2, but value surged 50% to a record ₹1.98 lakh crore on high prices. Jewellery demand fell 15-17% y/y to 75.1t as affordability weighed, while bar and coin investment demand rose 9% to 50.3t — investment demand continuing to outpace jewellery, extending the trend first flagged in the July 22 update. WGC flagged a >7% YTD INR depreciation and the import duty hike as the main demand headwinds; festive/wedding season (H2) expected to provide some support (source: newkerala.com).
