## Goldman Sachs Revised Nowcast — May 18, 2026

Source: Mining Weekly / Bloomberg / Canadian Mining Report (2026-05-18)

- Goldman Sachs **revised its central bank gold purchase nowcast upward** to **~50 tonnes/month** (12-month moving average as of March 2026), up from **29 tonnes/month** under the prior methodology.
- The upward revision was driven by gaps in UK trade data since August 2025 that had failed to capture gold outflows from London vaults, pointing to **unrecorded sovereign buying**.
- Goldman now expects central banks to average **~60 tonnes/month** through 2026.
- Goldman reiterated its **$5,400/oz gold price target** for end-2026.

## 2026 Full-Year Outlook

- **World Gold Council forecast**: ~850 tonnes for full year 2026 (similar to 2025)
- **Goldman Sachs forecast**: ~60 t/month (~720 t annualised), with possible acceleration
- **Key drivers**: Reserve de-dollarisation, geopolitical risk hedging, USD diversification
- **Leading buyer YTD**: Poland (+20 t+ through early 2026, part of a multi-year plan to reach 700 t)
- **Geopolitical context**: Elevated geoeconomic uncertainty driving EM central bank accumulation
