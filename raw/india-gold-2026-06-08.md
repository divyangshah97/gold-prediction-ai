## Import & Trade
India's gold import duty remains at 15% (10% BCD + 5% AIDC), raised from 6% on May 13, 2026. No new policy change on June 8. World Gold Council (WGC) forecasts Indian gold demand will decline by ~50–60 tonnes in 2026 (~10% y/y), citing the duty hike as primary factor. Listed jewellers' shares fell 2–17% in the weeks following the duty announcement. Large chain stores report sequential slowdown but resilience in bridal/wedding demand.

Higher duties historically increase unofficial (grey market) imports — WGC cautions that actual demand may be understated by official import data.

## Retail & Jewellery Demand
Physical demand remains deeply suppressed. The fortnight ended May 27 data showed ~7.5t demand vs ~25t year-ago (~-70% YoY). Post-June 8 global price drop could provide marginal relief to consumers (lower spot), but the 15% duty and PM Modi's appeal to defer purchases keep a structural headwind in place. Auspicious wedding season (Akha Teej already passed May 2026) limits near-term demand recovery catalyst.

## ETF & Investment Flows
Gold ETF AUM at ₹1.71 lakh crore (FY26 end, March 2026), up +191% y/y. Q1 2026 gold ETF inflows hit a record: net ₹316 billion in Q1 2026 (approximately 20t); ETF holdings rose to 115t total by end-Q1 2026; AUM increased to INR 1.7 trillion (~US$18.5 billion). Import duty does not affect ETF purchases (financial instrument, not physical import). ETF inflows remain the bright spot in India's gold market. May–June 2026 flows: moderation expected given overall price decline.

## INR Impact
USD/INR at ~95.19 on June 8 (slightly stronger vs 95.21 on June 5). DXY broke above 100 — the first time in 2026 — reflecting the strong US jobs data. A stronger DXY normally weakens the INR; however, the INR has been resilient at ~95 despite global USD strength, possibly due to RBI FX intervention. If DXY continues above 100, INR could weaken toward 96+, pushing MCX gold above ₹1,55,000–₹1,56,000/10g even if USD spot remains flat. The current MCX price of ₹1,52,720 reflects the gold price drop dominating the modest USD/INR effect.

## Policy & Regulation
No new gold policy announcements on June 8. 15% import duty structure unchanged. The Indian government continues to monitor import data for evidence of smuggling revival (WGC warning flagged). No new RBI gold reserve policy statements.

## Other India Gold News
Strong US May NFP (+172K vs 85K forecast) was the key global catalyst driving Indian gold lower today. India's gold market has an asymmetric sensitivity to USD strength — a DXY above 100 tends to create compounded pressure on MCX as both the USD spot decline AND the USD/INR weakness work together. However, on June 8 the INR has been relatively stable, so the MCX decline (~2%) is smaller than the global spot decline (~3.28%).
