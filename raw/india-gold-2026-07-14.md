## Import & Trade

The **15% import duty** (effective May 13, 2026 midnight notification) remains in force. Plus 1% AIDC + 3% GST = approximately **19% effective duty**.

- Monthly gold imports running at approximately **15–25 tonnes** (vs. 80–100t/month pre-duty baseline — a 70-75% collapse)
- Physical market showing a domestic discount of ~$100–150/oz vs. international parity
- No new government import duty announcement today
- July Union Budget window remains a potential catalyst for duty change — no announcement yet

## Retail & Jewellery Demand

Retail demand remains severely suppressed by the 15% import duty. Consumer sentiment has shifted from gold purchase to wait-and-see mode. Key observations:

- **WGC June 2026 update** ("Demand Cools"): India gold jewellery demand fell sharply in Q1 2026 with the high prices; Q2 expected to show further softening given new duty
- Multiple AMCs (HDFC, ICICI Pru, Nippon India, Tata AMC, Axis, Aditya Birla) have imposed gold ETF subscription restrictions on large investors since June 2026
- Festive season (Navratri–Diwali: October–November) is the next potential demand recovery window; however, current MCX levels (₹1,41,000) represent some improvement vs. the ₹1,47,720 June-July peak in terms of affordability
