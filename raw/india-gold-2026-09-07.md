## MCX Gold Price

MCX gold (Oct 2026 delivery) fell 0.33% to **₹1,52,270/10g** as of ~9:35 AM IST on Sept 7, down from the previous close of ₹1,54,154/10g (source: LatestLY/city-wise trackers). Retail spot: 24K ₹15,480/gram, 22K ₹14,190/gram, 18K ₹11,610/gram — largely stable/slightly down versus the Sept5 print where major jewellers (Joyalukkas, Malabar Gold & Diamonds) also quoted 24K at ₹15,480/gram. The pullback tracks the international COMEX retreat to Friday Sept4's close ($4,429.00, -1.14%) on the stronger-than-expected US August jobs report.

## Retail & Jewellery Demand

World Gold Council and retail-sector coverage (Business Standard, Sept 6) point to resilient jewellery demand heading into the extended festive season: H1 2026 India gold demand volumes were roughly flat y/y (281.50t, +1.8%) but total spend rose 72.5% y/y to ~₹4.25 lakh crore on higher prices. Analysts expect wedding- and festival-driven buying (through Diwali/Dhanteras) to support volumes in Q3-Q4 FY27, with a continued consumer shift toward lighter, lower-carat jewellery at large chains as smaller independent jewellers see softer footfall.
