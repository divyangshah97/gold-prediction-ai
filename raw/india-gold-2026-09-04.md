## Import & Trade

**New this window**: DGFT has extended the deadline for utilizing India-UAE CEPA tariff-rate-quota (TRQ) gold-import licences to **September 30, 2026** — an automatic extension requiring no amendment application or fee (source: whalesbook.com, thehansindia.com). This follows directly from Sept3's flagged story on the UAE's 1-point tariff-gap (14% FTA vs 15% MFN) driving increased UAE-routed gold imports — the extension gives importers more runway to use the preferential-rate quota (up to 200t/year at 1% duty concession) before it lapses. Standard 15% import duty regime (since May 13, 2026) remains otherwise unchanged; the government's reported review of a possible cut to 6% remains undecided.
