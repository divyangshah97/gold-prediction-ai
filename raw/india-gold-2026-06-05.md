## Import & Trade

- **15% import duty** (raised from 6% on May 13, 2026) remains in force — no change announced
- **Advanta Assured (AA) scheme** 100kg cap still active
- WGC May 2026 report ("Import tightening") notes: "mid-May to mid-June is considered inauspicious for buying gold" — seasonal weakness compounding duty-hike impact
- Full-year 2026 demand decline estimate: **50–60 tonnes (~10% y/y)** per WGC
- Physical demand in fortnight ended May 27: ~7.5t vs ~25t same period 2025 (**-70% YoY**)
- High duty historically revives grey market/smuggling channels; WGC flagged this risk

## Retail & Jewellery Demand

- Retail demand remains severely suppressed (~-70% YoY from fortnight ended May 27)
- Mid-May to mid-June is the traditional "shunyam" (inauspicious) period — no gold buying for weddings/auspicious purposes; amplifying duty-hike impact
- Wedding season picks up post-June 14 (Ashadha Ekadashi); test of whether demand recovery happens at higher duty levels
- PM Modi's May 10 public appeal to defer purchases still in effect — no reversal announced

## ETF & Investment Flows

- Q1 2026 (January–March): Record quarterly inflows of **₹316 billion** into Indian gold ETFs; holdings reached **115t**; AUM ₹1.71 lakh crore (FY26 end)
- April 2026 inflows: ₹30.4 billion (moderated from ₹240.5 billion January peak; 12th consecutive positive month)
- May 2026 flows: Not yet officially reported; expected to show further moderation due to high gold price and duty-hike uncertainty
- Gold ETFs are **insulated from the import duty** — they are financial instruments and do not trigger physical gold imports on each unit purchase
- The duty shock is accelerating the structural shift from physical gold (bars/coins/jewellery) to digital gold (ETFs/Sovereign Gold Bonds)
