## RBI / Reserves

RBI weekly data (week ended July 4, 2026, published mid-July, still the latest available): India's gold reserves value rose **$342 million** to **$84.846 billion**; total forex reserves **$699.736 billion** (source: DD News/RBI). Separately, RBI has cut its US Treasury security holdings to a **six-year low** as part of a broader reserve-diversification strategy, with gold accumulation as the offsetting leg (source: Organiser, July 14) — this is a continuation of the "de-dollarisation via reserves" narrative already tracked, not new since the last run. Physical gold holdings remain static at 880.52t; RBI continues to deny gold-sale rumours.

## Import & Trade

No new data since last run. 15% effective import duty (10% basic customs + 5% AIDC) remains in force since May 13, 2026. Consumer demand is recovering after the mid-May to mid-June lull (seasonally soft period + duty hike + PM's public appeal to moderate gold purchases). WGC's July "Mixed demand signals" note says buying has picked up in recent weeks, led by jewellery, while bar-and-coin demand has cooled. Manufacturers are already receiving festive-season order bookings from retailers ahead of the August festive period.
