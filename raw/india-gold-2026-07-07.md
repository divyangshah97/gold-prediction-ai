## Import & Trade

India's gold import duty remains at 15% (raised from 6% in May 2026). The duty hike roughly doubled the cost of importing one kilogram of gold.

Immediate impact:
- Physical demand plummeted ~70% immediately after the hike (India Bullion and Jewellers Association)
- Domestic market trading at a severe discount to official landed price (~$150/oz discount vs ~$14/oz pre-hike)
- No new policy changes announced on July 7

The WGC had estimated the 15% duty could reduce annual Indian gold demand by 50–60 tonnes.

## Retail & Jewellery Demand

Subdued. The 15% import duty shock continues to weigh on jewellery and physical bar/coin demand. Major retailers are absorbing some margin pressure to keep prices stable. No festive season catalyst imminent in July.

## ETF & Investment Flows

India recorded its first month of net gold ETF outflows in over a year (post duty hike in May 2026), reversing sustained inflows through early 2026. Q1 2026 had been a record quarter for Indian gold ETF demand (~20t net inflows).

HDFC Gold ETF reportedly imposed temporary restrictions on large institutional inflows during peak demand periods earlier in 2026 when it became too popular. AUM for Indian gold ETFs remains near ₹1.7T but momentum has turned negative.
