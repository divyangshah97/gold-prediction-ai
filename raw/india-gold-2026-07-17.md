## MCX Gold Price
MCX gold futures were trading in the range of ₹1,40,000–₹1,42,100 per 10 grams on July 17, 2026. BusinessToday reported "Yellow metal hits ₹1.40 lakh" with positive intraday bias (+0.08%). India.com reported MCX gold at ₹1,42,100 (down ₹1,378, −0.96%) in an earlier session. The wide intraday range reflects the USD/INR-amplified impact of COMEX gold spot falling to ~$3,985 (−1.8% from July 16 $4,059). Key level: ₹1,40,000 acting as intraday support; ₹1,42,000 as resistance.

## RBI / Reserves
No new RBI gold reserve data published today. RBI gold holdings remain at 880.52t as per last available data. RBI denied $12B gold sale in June 2026 (confirmed in prior updates). Repatriation of 168t from London ongoing but no new milestones announced.

## Import & Trade
No new import data for July. The government raised the gold import duty from 6% to 15% in May 2026 — this remains unchanged. WGC estimated this duty increase will reduce Indian gold demand by 50–60 tonnes annually. FY2026 total gold imports reached a record $71.98 billion before the duty hike took effect. Shipment disruptions from Dubai (due to Middle East conflict and Strait of Hormuz closure) could tighten near-term physical supply to India.

## Retail & Jewellery Demand
Jewellery demand continues to face headwinds from elevated prices (down −19% y/y in volume terms per WGC Q1 2026 data). Record spending in value terms (+47% y/y) cushions the volume decline. Monsoon season traditionally soft for jewellery; festive season buying (Navratri/Diwali, Oct–Nov) is the next major catalyst. The 15% import duty has pushed Indian retail gold prices significantly above international levels, further dampening consumer appetite.

## ETF & Investment Flows
Gold ETF AUM in India stood at ~₹1.84 trillion (May 2026), a record. Q1 2026 saw net gold ETF demand of 20t (also a record). AMC subscription caps were imposed on new ETF purchases given strong inflows. Investment demand has been substituting for jewellery demand at elevated price levels. No new July-specific ETF data available.

## INR Impact
USD/INR on July 17, 2026: ₹96.43 (up from ₹96.19 on July 16 — rupee weakened slightly by +0.25%). The INR has depreciated from ₹95.32 on July 10 to ₹96.43 today — a weakening of approximately 1.16% over the week. This INR depreciation partially cushions the decline in COMEX gold (in USD) when translated to MCX gold (in INR). COMEX gold fell −1.8% today but MCX gold's INR decline was smaller due to the weaker rupee. The Iran-driven oil surge (WTI ~$79.67/bbl) and associated current account pressure are contributing to INR weakness.

## Policy & Regulation
Gold import duty remains at 15% (unchanged since May 2026). No new government gold policy announcements today. RBI monetary policy: repo rate held at 5.25% (June 5 MPC decision, neutral stance). Next RBI MPC meeting schedule not yet confirmed for August.

## Other India Gold News
- The Iran-US conflict and Strait of Hormuz closure is affecting global gold shipping logistics. Dubai-routed gold shipments to India have been disrupted, with discounts to the London price narrowing sharply.
- Silver fell to ~₹2.15 lakh per kg (BusinessToday July 17), reflecting global silver weakness alongside gold.
