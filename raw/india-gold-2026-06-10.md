## ETF & Investment Flows

**Major development**: Gold ETF subscription restrictions have widened significantly.

- **Nippon India** (Gold BeES ETF + Gold Savings Fund): Capped at ₹10 lakh per PAN per month for FoF; halted direct fresh subscriptions >₹25 crore. Effect from ~June 5.
- **HDFC** (Gold ETF FoF): ₹10 lakh per PAN per calendar month cap on lump-sum purchases and switch-ins.
- **ICICI Prudential**: Direct subscriptions capped at ₹25 crore. Effective June 5, 2026.
- **Tata AMC**: Has also joined in capping fresh gold ETF inflows (latest entrant).
- **UTI Gold ETF FoF**: Also under restrictions.

**Why**: The government raised the import duty to reduce gold's drain on India's current account and rupee. Even though ETFs don't directly import gold on each unit purchase (they use an AP-mediated creation basket), large net ETF inflows eventually require physical gold creation units by APs, tightening the supply pipeline. Fund houses are acting in spirit of government policy to manage the import channel.

**Investment context**: India accounted for 32% of all global gold ETF demand in Q1 2026. The restrictions represent a significant structural shift — this is the first time India's major AMCs have simultaneously restricted gold ETF purchases.
