## Import & Trade
**MAJOR POLICY CHANGE (effective May 13, 2026):**
- India raised Basic Customs Duty (BCD) on gold from 5% to 10%; Agriculture Infrastructure and Development Cess (AIDC) also raised → total import duty now 15% (up from 6% previously).
- Govt also capped gold imports under Advance Authorisation (AA) scheme at 100 kg per importer (effective May 14), tightening norms against misuse by jewellery exporters.
- Motivation: Rising energy costs from Iran conflict, weakening INR at record lows; PM Modi urged citizens to pause gold purchases for one year.
- Impact: India gold demand fell to ~7.5t in the fortnight ended May 27 vs ~25t same period last year — a ~70% drop.
- Source: BusinessToday (May 31, 2026), CNBC (May 13, 2026)

## Policy & Regulation
- May 13: Import duty raised to 15% total (BCD 10% + AIDC 5%).
- May 14: Advance Authorisation cap at 100 kg/importer to prevent duty-free import misuse.
- PM Modi public statement urging citizens to pause gold purchases for one year to stabilise the rupee.
- These measures are the most aggressive India gold import restriction since the 2013 duty hike (which also had limited long-term demand impact).
