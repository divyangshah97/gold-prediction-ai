## Import & Trade
India's gold import duty raised from 6% to 15% in May 2026 (largest single increase on record; fully reversed the July 2024 duty cut). Policy driven by INR weakness and current account pressures. WGC (July 1) projects: higher duty will cut India gold demand by 50-60 tonnes in 2026 (~10% y/y decline). Imports confirmed at 25-30 tonnes/month (down ~70% from pre-duty baseline of ~100t/month). Inventory purchased at 6% duty still circulating through supply chain.
