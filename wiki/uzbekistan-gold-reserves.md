# Uzbekistan Gold Reserves

**Summary**: Uzbekistan is a top-5 central bank gold buyer globally in H1 2026. **UPDATE Aug 13**: Uzbekistan's central bank reported (Aug 10, 2026) gold+FX reserves rose ~$590M over the prior month, with gold holdings reaching **~430.8 tonnes as of Aug 1, 2026** (up from the ~366t figure tracked earlier in the year) — gold is now **~85-87% of total reserves**. **WGC Q2 2026 Gold Demand Trends (published Jul 30) confirms +16t in Q2 2026**, consistent with the April+May+June monthly prints already tracked here (YTD ~41t through June). Buying is driven by domestic gold production from state-owned Navoi Mining and a deliberate policy of retaining mined gold in reserves.

## Why Uzbekistan Buys

Uzbekistan is one of the world's top 10 gold producers. Its central bank (Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan) routinely purchases gold directly from domestic miners — a common strategy for resource-rich EM central banks to:

1. **Retain export revenues** in hard assets rather than converting all proceeds to USD/EUR
2. **Build reserve buffers** against currency volatility (Uzbek som is a managed float)
3. **[[De-dollarisation]]**: Reduce reliance on Western financial infrastructure

Uzbekistan's buying is largely a mechanical function of its mining output — as long as Navoi Mining produces, the central bank is likely to keep buying.

## Significance for Gold Price Prediction

- Uzbekistan is a **consistent, volume buyer** — 25t in a single quarter ranks it ahead of China's reported 7t
- Buying is **production-linked**, making it predictable barring a major shift in mining policy
- As an EM central bank buyer, it contributes to the structural floor described in [[central-bank-gold-demand]]
