# Poland Gold Reserves

**Summary**: Poland's National Bank is the largest single central bank buyer of gold in 2026 YTD. **Bloomberg July 9 CONFIRMED: 82t YTD 2026 (Governor Glapinski); total ~632t (revised up from ~613t — 595t April data + 37t since); ~100t stored domestically.** Gold = 30% of total FX reserves. Long-term target: 700t; NBP approved 150t additional purchase plan. NATO eastern flank security-driven. **No new July data today (July 16)**.

Poland was the **#1 central bank gold buyer globally in 2026 YTD**, with NBP Governor Adam Glapiński confirming **82 tonnes purchased in 2026** at a July 9 press conference in Warsaw (Bloomberg July 9, 2026). This is one of the most aggressive annual buying campaigns in NBP history. The bank accumulated **+37t** since the last official April 2026 data release (~595t), worth approximately **$5 billion** at current prices. Total reserves: **~632t** (595t April official + 37t through July 9 → 632t; some sources citing 632.4t; ~100t stored domestically). Note: phemex/panewslab cite 613t which appears to be the May-only total; the July 9 Glapinski press conference figure implies 632t. (source: Bloomberg July 9; cryptobriefing July 10; Kitco July 10 2026)

## Why Poland Is Buying

Poland's gold accumulation is explicitly strategic and geopolitical:

1. **NATO eastern flank security**: As the country on NATO's border with Russia/Belarus, Poland is building gold reserves as an extreme contingency asset — one that retains value and remains accessible even during severe geopolitical crises or financial sanctions scenarios.
2. **[[De-dollarisation]]**: Reducing exposure to EUR- and USD-denominated assets held in foreign custodians.
3. **Multi-year national policy**: The National Bank of Poland has publicly stated a target of 700 tonnes, making this a durable, predictable demand source over several years.

## Significance for Gold Price Prediction

Poland's buying is:
- **Persistent**: It is policy-driven with a clear long-term target, not reactive to gold prices
- **Sizeable**: 31t in a single quarter is among the largest single-country quarterly purchases globally
- **A leading indicator of EM security-driven demand**: Other NATO-adjacent countries may follow a similar strategy

As part of [[central-bank-gold-demand]], Poland's contribution is one of the cleaner, more predictable demand signals available.
