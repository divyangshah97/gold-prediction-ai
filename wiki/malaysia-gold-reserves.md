## Why Malaysia Is Buying

1. **[[De-dollarisation]]**: Malaysia has been vocal in ASEAN forums about reducing USD dependence in regional trade — gold accumulation is the reserve-side complement to this
2. **BRICS+ alignment**: Malaysia applied for BRICS+ membership in 2023; gold reserve building aligns with the BRICS reserve diversification narrative
3. **Regional trend**: Neighbour Indonesia (2t Q1 2026) is also buying; a Southeast Asian reserve diversification wave is emerging. See also [[cambodia-gold-reserves]]
4. **Currency protection**: Ringgit is a managed float; gold provides a non-USD anchor

## BRICS+ Context

Malaysia's gold buying fits the broader de-dollarisation theme being pursued by BRICS+ members. Malaysia has engaged with BRICS+ since 2023 and is oriented toward reducing USD-denominated reserve exposure — buying gold is consistent with this policy direction. See [[de-dollarisation]].
