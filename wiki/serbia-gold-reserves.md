## Why Serbia Is Buying

Serbia has been an active gold buyer since the mid-2010s and has stated publicly that gold is a key strategic reserve:

1. **Geopolitical neutrality hedge**: Serbia maintains ties with both Russia and the EU — gold is a reserve asset that stays out of either bloc's financial infrastructure
2. **EU candidate hedge**: As an EU membership candidate, Serbia faces uncertainty about eventual EUR adoption; gold provides a reserve anchor independent of the euro
3. **Historical preference**: Governor Jorgovanka Tabaković has made gold a stated reserve priority; accumulation is a consistent policy
4. **[[De-dollarisation]]**: Serbia is not a sanctions target, but observing Russia's 2022 asset freeze has reinforced the case for non-Western reserve assets

## Significance

Serbia's continued buying — even at 1t/quarter — reflects the **durability of gold as a strategic preference** across diverse geopolitical situations. Not just BRICS-aligned countries but EU-candidate countries are adding gold. See [[czech-republic-gold-reserves]] for a comparable NATO member example.
