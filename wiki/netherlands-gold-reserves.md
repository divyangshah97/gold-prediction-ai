# Netherlands (DNB) Gold Reserves

**Summary**: The Dutch central bank (De Nederlandsche Bank, DNB) relocated roughly 86 tonnes of gold from vaults in New York and Ottawa to London in 2026, citing "crisis preparedness" and geopolitical risk — a new entry in the wider theme of European central banks reassessing gold custody arrangements alongside [[germany-gold-reserves]], [[france-gold-reserves]], and [[italy-gold-reserves]].

**Sources**: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/netherlands-gold-transfer-us-canada-uk.html, https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/02/europe/dutch-central-bank-shifts-gold-to-britain-intl-hnk, https://fortune.com/2026/09/03/netherlands-gold-us-canada-geopolitics-unrest-europe/

**Last updated**: 2026-09-09

---

## Overview

DNB moved approximately 86 tonnes of gold out of vaults in New York and Ottawa to the Bank of England in London, executed gradually between March and August 2026. Only about 27 tonnes were physically shipped; the remainder was handled via sale/buyback swaps rather than physical transport. The stated rationale is "crisis preparedness" amid heightened geopolitical risk — a theme echoing (though distinct from) the repatriation-to-home-soil pressure seen in [[germany-gold-reserves]] and [[italy-gold-reserves]]; DNB's move is a reallocation *between* foreign custodians (US/Canada → UK), not a repatriation to Dutch soil.

## Post-move allocation

| Location | Share |
|---|---|
| New York (FRBNY) | 18.5% |
| Ottawa (Bank of Canada) | 18.5% |
| London (Bank of England) | 32.1% |
| Zeist, Netherlands (domestic) | 30.8% |

## Context

The Netherlands' gold reserve policy has historically diversified across four custodians (US, Canada, UK, and domestic vaults at Zeist) rather than concentrating holdings in any one location. This 2026 shift increases the London share materially, consistent with the UK's role as the world's largest gold custodian hub — see [[uk-gold-reserves]]. No change in total reported reserves has been identified; this is a custody relocation, not a purchase or sale.
