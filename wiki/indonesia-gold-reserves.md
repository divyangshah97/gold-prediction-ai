## Why Indonesia Is Buying

1. **Domestic production alignment**: Indonesia hosts the Grasberg mine (Freeport-McMoRan), one of the world's largest gold mines — similar to [[uzbekistan-gold-reserves]] and [[kazakhstan-gold-reserves]], Indonesia retains some mined gold domestically
2. **[[De-dollarisation]]**: Part of the broader ASEAN/EM trend; Indonesia has been cautious about USD reserve concentration since 2013 taper tantrum
3. **BRICS+ engagement**: Indonesia has engaged with BRICS+ forums — gold builds credibility as a reserve anchor

## Regional Context

Indonesia, [[malaysia-gold-reserves]], and [[cambodia-gold-reserves]] all bought gold in Q1 2026, representing a coherent Southeast Asian central bank gold accumulation pattern. This is a positive structural signal for [[central-bank-gold-demand]].
