## What This Source Covers

This is a compiled data file based on reporting from Mining Weekly, Bloomberg, Kitco News, World Gold Council, and Canadian Mining Report, covering the week ending May 18, 2026.

**Important caveat**: Central banks do not publish weekly gold purchase data. They report monthly with a 4–6 week lag. The most granular public data available as of this week is Q1 2026 (Jan–Mar), supplemented by the Goldman Sachs revised model published May 18, 2026.
