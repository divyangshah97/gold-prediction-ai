# France — Banque de France Gold Reserves

**Summary**: France holds **2,437 tonnes** of gold — the world's 4th largest reserve — and made headlines in April 2026 by completing a full withdrawal of all its gold from the US Federal Reserve, making **~$15 billion (€13B)** in the process. Every ounce of French sovereign gold now sits on French soil. This is the most significant Western central bank gold repatriation event since Germany's 2017 programme.

## The $15 Billion Repatriation — Full Story

This is one of the most financially clever central bank gold operations in recent history.

### What Happened

Between **July 2025 and January 2026**, the Banque de France:

1. **Sold 129 tonnes of gold** held at the FRBNY in New York — at or near record prices (~$3,500–$5,000/oz range during this period)
2. **Bought back the equivalent 129 tonnes** of gold on the European market — in newer, modern, internationally standardised bars
3. **Stored the new bars** in its own vaults in Paris (La Souterraine)

### The Profit

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| 2025 exceptional FX income from upgrade | **€11 billion** |
| Additional 2026 gains | **~€2 billion** |
| **Total gain** | **~€13 billion (~$15 billion)** |

The profit arose because the bars held in New York were **old, non-standard legacy bars** — pre-war era gold of varying purity and weight that did not meet modern LBMA Good Delivery standards. When sold at market price (~$4,000–5,000/oz at the time), their value reflected current market rates, not book value. The replacement bars bought in Europe were compliant with modern standards but cost slightly less per equivalent troy ounce (because new compliant bars trade at a tighter premium than legacy bars that require assaying/refining).

The Banque de France described the operation as a **"practical financial decision"** — an upgrade of asset quality, not a political statement. But the timing and outcome speak for themselves.

### What Remains to Be Done

- A further **134 tonnes** of French gold still needs to be "upgraded" (brought up to modern standard) — target completion **by 2028**
- This additional modernisation does not involve further US repatriation — just bar replacement/refinement

## Why This Matters Globally

### The Germany/Italy Pressure

France's $15B gain immediately triggered political and media pressure on Germany and Italy to do the same:

- **Germany** still holds ~1,236t at FRBNY — Medium (Apr 2026): "Germany and Italy Are Being Asked to Do the Same" (see [[germany-gold-reserves]])
- **Italy** still holds gold in New York and London alongside its Rome vault — political pressure growing (see [[italy-gold-reserves]])
- Both countries have legacy non-standard bars that could theoretically generate similar profits if sold and replaced

### The De-Dollarisation Signal

The Banque de France framed this as a financial upgrade — but the direction of travel is clear: a major Western central bank moved **all its sovereign gold from US custody to home soil**, at a time of:
- US foreign policy unpredictability under Trump 2.0
- Growing concern about dollar weaponisation via sanctions (see [[de-dollarisation]])
- Increasing global trend of repatriation (India, Germany, France — all reducing Western custodial exposure)

France became the first major Western central bank to hold **zero gold in the United States** since the Bretton Woods era.

## Historical Context

France has a long and proud history of gold independence:
- **1960s**: President de Gaulle famously demanded the US exchange France's dollars for gold, challenging dollar hegemony and contributing to the eventual end of Bretton Woods in 1971
- **Post-Bretton Woods**: France retained its large gold position while many countries sold (France never pursued a "Brown's Bottom"-style sale)
- **2017**: Germany completed its repatriation from Paris — France received the Paris-stored German gold back
- **2025–2026**: France goes one step further — repatriates from New York too, making money in the process

## Significance for Gold Price Prediction

1. **Static overall volume**: 2,437t in or out of New York doesn't change global gold supply — neutral for price
2. **Sentiment signal**: A major Western NATO ally moving ALL gold from US custody is a significant geopolitical confidence signal — **mildly bullish for gold** (confirms institutional preference for physical, sovereign-held gold)
3. **Precedent effect**: France's $15B profit creates a **financial template** for Germany and Italy to follow — if either does, it would further validate the home-custody trend
4. **Bar upgrade programme**: The 134t still being modernised means French gold supply to LBMA markets may fluctuate slightly as legacy bars are sold and replaced — negligible price effect but worth monitoring
