## Why Cambodia Is Buying

Cambodia is a highly dollarised economy (the USD is widely used domestically alongside the riel). Accumulating gold represents:

1. **Monetary sovereignty signalling**: Building gold reserves is a way for Cambodia to assert reserve independence despite high domestic USD circulation
2. **Regional alignment**: Mirrors the buying patterns of larger ASEAN peers [[malaysia-gold-reserves]] and [[indonesia-gold-reserves]]
3. **Dollar-risk hedge**: Gold protects the central bank's balance sheet against USD depreciation

## Significance

Cambodia buying 2t in a single quarter is notable for the size of its economy. On a reserves-as-% basis, this represents a meaningful allocation increase. It signals that gold accumulation is occurring even in smaller frontier/emerging markets — broadening the [[central-bank-gold-demand]] base.
