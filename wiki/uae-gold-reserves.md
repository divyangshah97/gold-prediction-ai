## Why the UAE Is Buying

1. **[[De-dollarisation]]**: The UAE dirham is pegged to the USD. Building gold reserves is a policy hedge against long-term USD peg vulnerability — especially as the UAE deepens ties with China, India, and BRICS+ countries
2. **BRICS+ alignment**: UAE joined BRICS+ in January 2024 — gold reserve building is consistent with BRICS+ members' diversification away from USD
3. **Iran context**: UAE is directly exposed to Strait of Hormuz disruption ([[iran-conflict-2026]]). Gold is a geopolitical hedge against regional escalation
4. **Gold hub synergy**: The UAE's physical gold trading infrastructure (DMCC, Dubai refineries, Gold Souk) means the central bank has privileged access to physical gold at minimal premium

## The Broader UAE Gold Role

Beyond central bank reserves, the UAE is critical to global physical gold flows:
- Dubai is a key re-export hub routing gold from producing countries (Africa, CIS) to consuming markets (India, China)
- UAE refineries refine gold from conflict-prone areas that Western refiners won't handle
- This makes UAE an important node in the **[[hidden-sovereign-buying]]** pipeline — some flows through Dubai may not be fully captured in Western trade statistics

## Significance for Gold Price Prediction

UAE's 1t central bank purchase is modest. The more important UAE factor is its role as a **physical gold routing hub** — any disruption to Dubai's gold trade infrastructure (e.g., from Hormuz escalation) would tighten physical gold supply globally.
