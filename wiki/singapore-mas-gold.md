## Gold Trading Hub Strategy

Singapore announced in 2026 its plan to launch **central bank gold vaulting services in October 2026** — a significant strategic development positioning Singapore alongside London, Zurich, and New York as a major gold custodian and trading centre. The Monetary Authority of Singapore outlined key focus areas to develop Singapore as a gold trading centre, encompassing:

1. Physical gold trading infrastructure
2. Gold-linked financial products
3. Regulatory framework for gold market participants
4. International gold custodial services

This positions Singapore to attract gold flows from ASEAN central banks and private investors who currently use London or Swiss vaults.

## Historical Context

MAS was an active buyer of gold from 2021 through mid-2024, significantly building up reserves during the post-COVID monetary uncertainty period. The pause from October 2025 through April 2026 reflected portfolio rebalancing amid gold's price surge (gold reached $5,589/oz on January 28, 2026). The May 2026 resumption of +4t coincides with gold's correction from ATH (gold at ~$4,163 on July 6, 2026 — down ~25% from peak).
