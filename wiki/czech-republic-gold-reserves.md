## Why Czech Republic Is Buying

The Czech National Bank is unusual among EU member states in having a **publicly stated gold accumulation strategy**. Most EU central banks hold gold passively (Germany, France, Italy, Netherlands inherited large positions from Bretton Woods era).

Key drivers:
1. **Governor Aleš Michl's policy**: CNB Governor has publicly stated gold is a hedge against "extreme risks" and the bank intends to build up reserves substantially
2. **Starting from a low base**: Czech Republic held only ~10–15t for years — it is in catch-up mode
3. **Not yet in EUR zone**: Without ECB oversight of reserves, CNB has more freedom to allocate to gold
4. **NATO eastern flank**: Like [[poland-gold-reserves]], Czech Republic has Russia-adjacent security concerns driving reserve diversification into non-confiscatable assets

## Significance for Gold Price Prediction

- The CNB's programme is a **multi-year demand pipeline** — 5t/quarter pace sustained would mean 20t/year
- If the 100t target is confirmed, Czech Republic alone represents ~60–65t of remaining demand to execute
- Represents a broader trend: **smaller EU NATO members building gold positions**, following Poland's lead
