## Why Kazakhstan Buys

Kazakhstan holds one of the highest gold-to-reserves ratios of any central bank globally. The National Bank of Kazakhstan (NBK) has a formal programme of purchasing domestically mined gold:

1. **Domestic gold purchase programme**: NBK buys directly from miners at market price — retaining export value within Kazakhstan
2. **High gold concentration**: With ~65–70% of reserves in gold, Kazakhstan is one of the most gold-heavy central banks globally
3. **Geopolitical hedging**: Russia-adjacent; maintains strategic autonomy through non-Western reserve assets
4. **[[De-dollarisation]]**: Reduces USD exposure in reserves

## Historical Context

Kazakhstan has been a regular buyer for over a decade and is one of the most reliable EM central bank gold buyers globally. Unlike some countries whose buying is reactive to geopolitical events, Kazakhstan's programme is structural.

## Significance for Gold Price Prediction

- **Reliable, predictable demand**: Production-linked buying makes Kazakhstan a stable contributor to [[central-bank-gold-demand]]
- **Volume**: 12t/quarter is meaningful at the global level
- **High gold concentration**: Kazakhstan is unlikely to reduce its gold preference even at elevated prices
