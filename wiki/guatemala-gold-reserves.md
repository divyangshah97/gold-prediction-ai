# Guatemala Gold Reserves

**Summary**: Banco de Guatemala bought 2 tonnes of gold in Q1 2026, one of the few Latin American central banks actively adding to gold reserves — a small but notable signal of EM gold accumulation extending into Central America.

## Why Guatemala Is Buying

1. **USD reserve hedge**: Guatemala's quetzal is pegged to the USD — gold provides an off-peg reserve asset in case of USD instability
2. **Small nation resilience**: For smaller economies, gold is a non-confiscatable reserve that cannot be frozen via sanctions or financial infrastructure controls
3. **Latin American trend**: Mexico and Brazil have historically held gold; Guatemala's buying may reflect regional peer awareness
4. **No known domestic gold production link** — this appears to be a deliberate policy allocation, not a production-retention mechanism (needs source verification)

## Significance

Guatemala's 2t purchase is small in absolute terms but meaningful as a signal: **Central American central banks are now part of the global gold accumulation wave**. This broadens the [[central-bank-gold-demand]] buyer base beyond Asia and Eastern Europe.
