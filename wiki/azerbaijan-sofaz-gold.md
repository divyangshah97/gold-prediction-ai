## Why SOFAZ Is Selling

SOFAZ (State Oil Fund of the Republic of Azerbaijan) is not a central bank in the traditional sense — it is a sovereign wealth fund managing Azerbaijan's hydrocarbon revenues. Its gold holdings are managed as part of a broader multi-asset portfolio:

1. **Oil revenue cycle management**: When oil prices are lower or fiscal needs are higher, SOFAZ rebalances by reducing gold (converting to cash/bonds for domestic spending)
2. **Portfolio rebalancing**: After gold's strong price appreciation in 2025–2026, SOFAZ may be trimming to rebalance target allocation percentages
3. **Not a strategic reserve**: Unlike the National Bank of Azerbaijan's monetary reserves, SOFAZ's gold is a wealth fund allocation — more responsive to portfolio logic than geopolitical hedging

## Why This Is Not Bearish for Gold

Similar to [[russia-gold-reserves]] and [[turkey-gold-reserves]], SOFAZ's selling is:
- **Portfolio-driven**, not a view on gold prices
- **Transient**: Sovereign wealth funds cycle in and out of gold with oil revenues
- **Fully absorbed**: 22t from SOFAZ is offset by Poland's 31t alone in Q1 2026

SOFAZ sold gold and gold prices still rose — the buyer pool ([[central-bank-gold-demand]] + investment demand) fully absorbed the supply.
